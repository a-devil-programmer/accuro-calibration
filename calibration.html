Metrology & Calibration Services

Traceable field calibration designed to protect product quality, satisfy audit requirements, and minimize plant downtime.

1. Temperature Calibration & Mapping

What We Do:

We calibrate RTDs, thermocouples, temperature transmitters, digital thermometers, and temperature controllers using high-precision portable dry blocks and liquid baths. We also perform spatial thermal mapping for environmental chambers, autoclaves, cold rooms, and ovens.

What the Discipline Means:

Temperature calibration verifies that your thermal sensors read true thermal values against national/international standards (NIST/NABL traceable). Thermal mapping evaluates temperature distribution across a 3D volume to identify hot or cold spots.

How It Affects Your Process:

Even minor thermal drift can ruin product batches in food processing, pharmaceutical manufacturing, or chemical synthesis. Improper thermal mapping can result in unsterilized zones or uneven curing, leading to compliance failures, safety risks, and expensive batch rejections.

2. Pressure Calibration

What We Do:

On-site testing and calibration of analog pressure gauges, digital pressure indicators, pressure transmitters, safety relief valves, and pressure switches across vacuum to high-pressure pneumatic/hydraulic ranges.

What the Discipline Means:

Pressure metrology measures force per unit area exerted by fluids or gases. Calibration adjusts instruments to ensure true pressure readings relative to reference standards.

How It Affects Your Process:

Inaccurate pressure gauges compromise system safety and product quality. Over-pressurization risks pipe ruptures, vessel bursts, and plant shutdown, while under-pressurization leads to inefficient reactions and inconsistent yield.

3. Weight & Mass Calibration

What We Do:

Field calibration and verification for bench scales, platform scales, hopper/tank weighing systems, and industrial balances using certified class test weights.

What the Discipline Means:

Mass calibration establishes the absolute accuracy of weighing devices, ensuring load cells and mechanical balances respond linearly across their operational range.

How It Affects Your Process:

Mass errors compound quickly during batching and raw material mixing. Inaccurate scales cause recipe variations, incorrect product dosing, material waste, and financial loss in trade-billing applications.

4. Process Loop Calibration

What We Do:

End-to-end signal testing of 4–20 mA current loops, 0–10 V signals, I/P converters, positioners, and PLC/DCS input channels using multi-function calibrators.

What the Discipline Means:

Process calibration verifies the entire measurement loop—from field sensor to controller output—ensuring electrical control signals accurately reflect physical process parameters.

How It Affects Your Process:

A calibrated sensor is useless if loop signal loss causes the control system to misinterpret the reading. Loop calibration ensures control valves and actuators respond correctly, stabilizing plant operations and preventing runaway control loops.
